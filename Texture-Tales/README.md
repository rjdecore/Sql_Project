# Texture Tales — Sales & Product Analysis

## Business Objective
Analyze sales transactions to understand revenue, discount impact, membership value, product performance, and customer basket behavior.

## Business Questions
- What is total quantity and revenue?
- What is the total discount given?
- How many unique transactions are there?
- What is average basket size?
- How does average revenue differ between members and non-members?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products lead each segment/category?
- How do discounts affect revenue?

## SQL Techniques
- Joins
- Aggregations
- CTEs
- Window functions
- Member vs non-member segmentation
- Product and category ranking

## Tech Stack
**MySQL | SQL | CTEs | Window Functions | Customer Analytics | Sales Analysis**

## Findings & Business Value
The queries are designed to quantify sales, discounts, basket size, member/non-member differences and top products by segment. Use the query outputs to state exact leaders and totals; do not assume discounts cause higher revenue without a suitable comparison design.

## Business Value
Retail teams can review product mix, member engagement and discount allocation, and identify questions for promotion testing.
